Engineered Automation Solutions

Solution 01

CNC Machine Tending

“Maximize uptime & keep chips flying”

CNC equipment is one of the most costly capital assets, when sitting idle, in the entire industry. Waiting for an operator to load parts for repetitive production is very costly and not competitive. We deploy robotic work cells that automate the loading, unloading, staging and even inspection of components.

  • Dramatically Higher Machine Utilization & Throughput.
  • Cuts Labor Dependency.
  • Consistency, Quality & Lower Scrap.
Solution 02

Measurement & Inspection Automation

“Maximize product out the door with sacrificing quality”

Quality control is one of the most expensive process steps because it adds no physical value to the product—yet it is essential. Manual measurement and inspection consume skilled labor hours, slow production flow, and introduce human variability that lead to errors. Automating QC eliminates the non-value-added labor cost, accelerate cycle times, and dramatically increase the volume of good product that ships.

  • Higher Throughput & More Product Shipped.
  • Superior Consistency & Quality
  • Dramatically Lower Cost
Solution 03

Inline Inspections

“Check parts immediately after value added process”

By measuring and verifying parts in-line (on the machine, in the cell, or between stations), manufacturers catch defects immediately, reduce scrap and rework, free operators from repetitive checks, and increase the number of good parts shipped. The result is lower cost per part, higher throughput, and more consistent quality without adding a separate inspection bottleneck.

  • Catch defects immediately so bad parts never move downstream or reach the customer.
  • Cut the labor and equipment cost of a separate inspection step that adds no value to the part.
  • Increase good parts shipped by reducing scrap, rework, and process delays.
